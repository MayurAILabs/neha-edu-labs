About Neha Badgujar

Assistant Professor · M.Tech in VLSI Design · 8+ years of teaching Electronics, Embedded Systems, VLSI and Artificial Intelligence — and the educator behind Neha Edu Labs.

The Educator

A career built on clarity, outcomes and curiosity

Neha Badgujar is an Assistant Professor with an M.Tech in VLSI Design and over 8 years of classroom and lab teaching experience across Electronics, Digital & Analog Electronics, Embedded Systems and VLSI Design. Her teaching style blends rigorous fundamentals with an outcome-based approach — every module is designed around what a student should be able to do, not just recall.

Over the years she has mentored over a thousand engineering students, guided them through lab-heavy subjects like 8051 microcontrollers and analog circuit design, and helped many transition confidently into placements and higher studies. She is ISTQB certified in Software Testing, trained in Outcome Based Education (OBE) frameworks, and has spent the last few years actively expanding her own skill set into Python, SQL, Power BI and practical Artificial Intelligence — convinced that tomorrow's engineers need both strong fundamentals and modern tooling.

Neha Edu Labs was born out of a simple observation: good notes, structured roadmaps and honest mentorship are hard to find in one place. This platform is her attempt to fix that — for free, for anyone who wants to learn.

Research

Research Interests

Beyond the classroom, Neha Badgujar stays engaged with active research areas in signal processing, hardware design and embedded systems — bringing current, real-world context into every course she teaches.

ADC (Analog-to-Digital Conversion)

Exploring converter architectures, resolution/speed trade-offs and practical design considerations for real-world signal acquisition.

DAC (Digital-to-Analog Conversion)

Studying DAC architectures and their role in reconstructing accurate analog signals from digital data.

Noise Cancellation

Investigating active and passive noise cancellation techniques for cleaner signal processing in embedded systems.

Embedded Systems

Working with 8051-based and modern microcontroller platforms for real-time, resource-constrained applications.

VLSI Design

Focused on chip-level design fundamentals, mixed-signal circuits and the practical path from schematic to silicon.

Selected Focus Areas

  •   Noise mitigation strategies in embedded signal chains
  •   8051 and microcontroller-based real-time system design
