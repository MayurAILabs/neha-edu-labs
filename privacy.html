Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026. This policy explains what information Neha Edu Labs collects and how it is used.

1. Introduction

Neha Edu Labs ("we", "us", "our") is a free educational website offering courses, notes, projects, blog articles and curated AI & education news to students. This Privacy Policy explains what limited information we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

Neha Edu Labs is a static, no-account website. We do not require sign-up or login to access any course, note, project or article. The only information we may collect includes:

  •   Newsletter email address — if you voluntarily submit your email through the newsletter subscription form.
  •   Contact form details — your name, email address, subject and message if you use the Contact page.
  •   Basic technical data — standard web server logs (such as browser type and page requests) that may be collected automatically by our hosting provider.

Important note: the current version of this website is a front-end-only, static site with no live backend server processing newsletter or contact form submissions. Form data entered is handled entirely in your browser for demonstration purposes and is not currently transmitted to or stored on any server. This policy is a template that will be updated to accurately reflect data handling once a backend or third-party form service is connected.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. This site uses your browser's localStorage only to remember your preferred theme (light or dark mode) so that your preference persists between visits. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

4. Third-Party Services

To deliver fonts, icons, animations and interactivity, this website loads resources from the following third-party content delivery networks (CDNs):

  •   Google Fonts (Inter, Poppins typefaces)
  •   Font Awesome (icon library)
  •   AOS — Animate On Scroll (scroll animation library)
  •   Typed.js (typing animation effect on the homepage)

These providers may process standard technical request data (such as IP address) as part of delivering their resources, in accordance with their own privacy policies. This site also displays curated links to third-party AI and education news articles; visiting those external sites is subject to their respective privacy practices.

5. Children's Privacy

Neha Edu Labs is an educational resource intended for engineering students and general learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

6. Data Retention

Since this site currently has no live backend, no newsletter or contact submissions are retained on our servers. Once a backend or third-party email/form service is integrated, retained data (such as a subscribed newsletter email) will be kept only as long as necessary to provide the service, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  •   Ask what information (if any) we hold about you
  •   Request correction or deletion of your information
  •   Unsubscribe from newsletter communications at any time
  •   Clear your browser's localStorage at any time to remove locally stored theme preference

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, particularly as new features (such as a live backend, user accounts, or analytics) are added. The "Effective Date" at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the Contact page or email us at hello@nehaedulabs.in.
