Career Corner

Get placement-ready, not just exam-ready

Concrete, actionable guidance on resumes, interviews, placements, internships and what comes after your degree — no generic fluff.

Resume Tips

  •   Lead with projects, not just coursework
  •   Quantify impact where possible (accuracy, speed, users)
  •   Keep it to one page for under 3 years of experience

Interview Tips

  •   Practice explaining your own projects out loud, clearly
  •   Revise fundamentals — gates, OOPs, DBMS, OSI model
  •   Ask thoughtful questions back at the end

Placement Preparation

  •   Start aptitude & reasoning practice a semester early
  •   Mock group discussions and technical rounds with peers
  •   Research the company and role before every interview

Internships

  •   Apply early — many core-branch internships open by mid-year
  •   A focused 6-week internship beats an unfocused 6-month one
  •   Document what you built — it becomes resume material

Higher Studies (GATE / M.Tech / MS Abroad)

  •   Decide GATE vs. MS vs. placements by 3rd year, not last minute
  •   For GATE, prioritize previous years' papers over passive revision
  •   For MS abroad, start SOP drafts and LOR conversations a year ahead

Building a Portfolio

  •   Host 3-4 solid projects on GitHub with clear README files
  •   Deploy at least one project live so recruiters can click it
  •   Write a short post explaining the "why", not just the "what"
